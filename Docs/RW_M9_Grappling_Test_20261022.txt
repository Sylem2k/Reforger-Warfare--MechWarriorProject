FIRING EVENT HOOK
The script tries SCR_MuzzleEffectComponent.GetOnWeaponFired() first,
if the inherited M9 has that scripted muzzle effect, and also subscribes
to the equipped character EventHandlerManagerComponent OnProjectileShot.
Duplicate callbacks for one round are ignored.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- NOT compiled in Enfusion Workbench yet; engine API and runtime still
  require verification.
- The player may not be pulled if the character controller cancels native
  physics velocity. Check the console for [RW GRAPPLE] logs.
- If I get "Native player motion blocked" I may temporarily switch
  Scripted Position Fallback to ON in an isolated test world. That uses
  SetOrigin and can snap the first-person camera or clip collision.
- Surface acquisition uses weapon aiming direction when exposed by the
  player/weapon AimingComponent. Otherwise it falls back to character
  facing and needs further correction before release.
- Movement collision checks are only a first prototype, not full capsule
  sweeps or safe rooftop climbing. Falling can still hurt the player.
- Changing weapon during pull stops it automatically.
- The second click fires another normal pistol round: use dummy ammo
  before normal multiplayer use.
- This hook does not require any extra weapon interaction action,
  and does not change the existing firearm ADS input binding.

23 OCT 2026 - WALL IMPACT SAFETY / CONTROLLED ARRIVAL
-----------------------------------------------------
I confirmed the initial 1m lift, the actual pull at 18m/s, and a
normal stop near the anchor in my test log. However I then died from
being pulled directly into the wall. My old 2m stop distance was
measured from the chest to a POINT raycast hit, without slowing the
player or accounting for their body collision.

I updated my Grappling M9 to try a SAFE ARRIVAL prototype:
- Keep 100m maximum targeting range.
- Keep 1m initial lift where overhead space is clear.
- Keep max 18m/s travel speed over the long part of the pull.
- Start reducing speed inside 14m of the impact point.
- Stop moving horizontally at least 4.5m short of the impact point.
- Check forward clearance with 3 traces from foot/chest/head,
  including a buffer in the direction of movement.
- If the path gets too close to the wall, immediately brake and
  begin a controlled descent instead of driving toward the wall.
- During descent, keep horizontal velocity at zero and apply at most
  2.5m/s of downward velocity until I am close to walkable ground.
- Extend active pull duration 6s -> 12s because the last metres
  now use gradual deceleration at the configured 100m range.
- Allow up to 90s of controlled descent for very tall structures,
  with manual second-click cancel still available.

CAVEATS:
This is a WALL-IMPACT SAFETY PROTOTYPE, not an automatic ledge climb.
If I hook the side of a tall wall, I stop away from it and descend to
the nearest walkable ground directly below, instead of being teleported
onto the rooftop. A future ledge/roof-top traversal feature needs a
separate validated path that cannot phase my character into geometry.
Cancelling manually in midair may still allow an ordinary fall.
Rapid changes in player-controller physics, fall damage or other mods
might still create injuries. The new code is committed and structurally
checked but NOT compiled or tested inside Workbench.
