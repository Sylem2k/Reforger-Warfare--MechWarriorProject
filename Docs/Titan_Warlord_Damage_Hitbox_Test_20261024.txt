TITAN WARLORD DAMAGE / HITBOX RESTORATION — TEST NOTES
24 October 2026
Branch: fix/warlord-damage-hitboxes-test-20261024

I identified why the Warlord appeared invulnerable:
- My original Titan hitbox ZIP had seventeen CGS_HitBoxZone prefabs, but none were referenced in the active Titan_WarLord_OPFOR.et.
- The Warlord's single root RigidBody only had a 2.5 x 2.5 x 3.5 Vehicle box near its origin, not a full silhouette hitbox.
- The hull itself has 40,000 health, a 0.05 kinetic multiplier and a 0.65 explosive multiplier.
- The shield has 30,000 capacity with 750/s regeneration after 7 seconds; the boss caps fully counted small arms at 60/s and heavy damage at 2200/s. Those values are deliberately unchanged for the first hitbox test.

I added all seventeen hitbox resources under Prefabs/Vehicles/Mech/HitBoxes/Titan/:
body chest, torso, pelvis, head, weak spot (3x), 3 each on the two arms, and 3 each on the two legs.
The body boxes are attached to the root, head to the head entity, arm boxes to separate left/right cannon-arm prefabs, and leg boxes to the boss leg entities with J1/J2/J3 pivots.

The ZIP did not contain the original attachment coordinates and some PhysicsBoxGeometry extents were far larger than the visible mech. I chose smaller TEST extents and provisional offsets. Those are estimates, not a verified reconstruction of the original alignment. I must inspect them in Workbench and adjust before use on the production server.

Test diagnostics:
- Hull BFA_MechHitZone m_bLogDamage = 1
- CGS_MechBossComponent m_bDebug = 1 (10-second server damage summaries)
- m_bLogHits = 1 for BODY CHEST, BODY HEAD, LEGL SHIN and WEAKSPOT only
- Always-visible, brighter green Warlord shield bubble
- Shield HP, regen, damage multipliers, boss caps and weapon damage are unchanged

My test order:
1. Verify the prefab compiles and all seventeen child hitbox slots actually spawn.
2. In Game Master or Workbench, inspect the collision boxes from front, side and rear. Check that each limb box follows its movement and that the weak spot can be hit independently.
3. Shoot the upper chest with a rifle, then an RPG/vehicle cannon. Check [CGS HITBOX], [BFA DAMAGE], [CGS BOSS], and shield output in the SERVER log.
4. Repeat on a shin/arm, then aim at the rear weak spot. Confirm health transfer to the leg/arm part and to the hull, with 3x pre-armour damage only on the weak spot.
5. Verify shield health decreases even while hull health remains nearly unchanged, and test what happens once the shield is down.
6. Disable verbose damage logs before testing with 100–128 players. Balance shields and caps only AFTER the hit routing is confirmed.

I must not merge this branch into main until the collision shape, forwarding and multiplayer checks pass.
