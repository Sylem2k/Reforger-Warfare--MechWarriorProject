CGS COMPANION — DIRECT INTERACTION FIX (2026-10-21)
====================================================

WHY FOLLOW ME WAS MISSING:
The old test only added companion actions to Light_Mech_Frame_B and
Med_Mech_Frame_B. The Mech Bay also orders SampleMech, Med_Mech_Base,
both Heavy Judiciar variants, and both Assault Gravemarch variants.
Those six had no companion brain or interaction actions at all.

FIX:
All EIGHT friendly Mech Bay frames now expose two normal interaction
actions (context "default"), with VisibilityRange 20 metres:
  Mech: FOLLOW ME
  Mech: GUARD HERE

They do not use Arma's squad-command menu, a squad leader, a GM commander,
or a command-rank permission. Approach the physical mech on foot and
use the normal interaction menu (usually hold F) to choose the action.

Each mech can be claimed by a different friendly player. The Core
validates the player's actual ID, faction and distance on the server,
then keeps that owner assigned to that mech until disconnect.

REQUIRES paired Core branch:
  fix/companion-action-visibility-20261021

BEFORE MERGE:
- Load both fix branches in Workbench, compile scripts, and test in a
  dedicated multiplayer session.
- Spawn each Bay frame and check the action prompt from an ordinary
  non-commander infantry player, within 20 metres.
- Order FOLLOW and GUARD, then board and dismount to test AI handoff.
- Verify AI weapon groups continue attacking while companion is autonomous.
- Check two players controlling different mechs independently.
- Check a different player cannot claim an already owned mech.
- Test faction mismatch and disconnect fallback.

This patch does NOT modify enemy AI prefabs, weapon effects, HUD, or
gameplay firing, and does NOT add laser-designation missile support.
Only sample/friendly Mech Bay frame prefabs are changed.
Main remains untouched until validated.
