  7 seconds recharge delay, 20 seconds after-break delay.
- Shield bleed-through 3%. EMP drains shields faster (cost x3).

- Shield restored once at 66% and 33% remaining hull health.
- Titan leg part health 800 -> 10,000 each.
- Titan cannon / saw arm part health 600 -> 8,000 each.

WEAPONS
- Both giant arm slots match the existing LAS_CANNON module ID and
  reuse their actual visible arms; left / right default module ID 1.
- Both arm mounts yaw -125 to +125 degrees and pitch -70 to +85.
  The new traverse limits do NOT prove the model can clear its hull;
  I must inspect mesh clipping, downward alignment and joint axes.
- Titan arm las cannon damage 75 -> 230; range 1,500 -> 2,400m;
  RPM 300 -> 380; penetration 18 -> 38%; heat per shot 10 -> 14.
- Four autonomous anti-air flak modules scan aircraft up to 1,800m,
  aim vertically from -35 to +88 degrees, and lead targets with
  1,050m/s shells. Each burst has 6 flak shots, 320 max splash damage
  in a 12m radius, plus its normal spread/settle rules.
- AA has min altitude 5m (so hovering low Valkyries are still eligible
  while ordinary grounded vehicles are rejected).
- Two automatic point-defense units watch for incoming missiles
  within 150m, protect 44m around the Titan, carry 18 charges each,
  and refill a charge every 1.5s.
- Stomp watches for hostiles within 45m, charges for 2s, throws/
  knocks down targets in a 65m radius, damages enemy vehicles up to
  450 and infantry up to 75, and waits 12s between activations.
- Autonomous AA / stomp / point-defense do NOT need a human crew.
  These modules are still subject to original damage, faction, heat
  and server authority code from the old CGS core.

slots (around X +-7.4, model Y 21.75) rather than verified rig-space
aim pivots. The rear AA pair is initially at X +-7.35,Y21.5,Z-6.8;
point defense near X +-9,Y8.5,Z3. I must adjust them in Workbench
to match the actual 3D model and avoid clipping.
